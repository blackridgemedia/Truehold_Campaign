Campaign Notes
— Monitor the Week 2 Monday Lead Gen Ad daily. Pause and reallocate to Friday if cost per lead exceeds $30.
— Don't over-edit candid and team photos. Authenticity is the point — raw, real images outperform polished ones for trades on Instagram.
— If Week 1 Monday performs strongly (low CPM, high engagement), add $50–100 before Friday. A bigger retarget pool = better Week 2 results.
— Repurpose all posts to Stories with a link sticker. Team and candid photos get tapped through more than construction shots.
— Post at 7–9am or 6–8pm AEST Mon / Wed / Fri for best organic reach with Illawarra local audiences.
— Reply to every comment within the first hour — especially on team and candid posts, which attract the most questions. Early replies signal to the algorithm to keep distributing.
