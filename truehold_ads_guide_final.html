Before You Start
What You Need to Have Ready
Go through this checklist before opening Meta Ads Manager. You'll need all of these in place — missing any one of them will stop you mid-setup.
Checklist
✓
A Facebook Page for Truehold Retaining — the ads run through a Facebook Page, not a personal account. If Truehold doesn't have one, create it at facebook.com/pages/create before anything else.
✓
Instagram account connected to that Facebook Page — go to the Facebook Page settings → Linked Accounts → connect Truehold's Instagram. The ads will show on Instagram through this connection.
✓
A Meta Business Suite account — go to business.facebook.com and create one using the client's Facebook Page. This is where Ads Manager lives.
✓
Payment method added — inside Meta Business Suite go to Billing → Payment Methods → Add. Use the client's credit/debit card. Meta charges after the ads run.
✓
The project reel video file ready — downloaded and saved on your computer, ready to upload. Should be MP4 format.
✓
All construction and candid photos ready — organised in a folder. Know which ones you're using for each post before you start.
✓
Captions copied and ready to paste — open the campaign plan doc alongside this one, so you can copy-paste captions directly.
⚠
Important: You'll need to be an Admin on the Truehold Facebook Page to run ads. If you're the agency, ask the client to add you as an Admin via Facebook Page Settings → Page Roles.
Section 1 of 6
Getting into Ads Manager
How to navigate to Meta Ads Manager and understand what you're looking at before creating anything.
Go to this URL
adsmanager.facebook.com
Or from Facebook: click the grid icon (top right) → Ads Manager.
💡
If it asks you to choose a Business Account, select the one you created for Truehold Retaining. If it shows your personal ad account, look for a dropdown at the top left and switch to the correct business account.
What you'll see
A dashboard with Campaigns, Ad Sets, and Ads tabs across the top. Don't worry about what's in here yet — you're just making sure you're in the right place with the right account selected.
Meta Ads Manager has three levels. Every ad you create lives inside all three:
Campaign
The overall goal — e.g. "Awareness" or "Leads". You'll create one campaign per ad objective.
Ad Set
The audience and budget — who sees it, how much you spend, where it shows (Instagram Feed, Reels, etc).
Ad
The actual creative — the video or photos, caption, and CTA button that the person sees on Instagram.
💡
For this campaign you'll create 3 campaigns total — one for Awareness (Posts 01 + 03), one for Leads (Post 04), and one final retarget (Post 06). Each section below walks you through one paid post at a time.
Ads Manager → Settings → Ad Account Settings
Currency
AUD (Australian Dollar)
Time Zone
Australia/Sydney (AEDT)
⚠
You can only set currency once. Make sure it's AUD before you spend anything — you can't change it after the account has run any ads.

Section 6 of 6
Monitoring the Campaign
What to check, when to check it, and when to take action.
Reach
How many unique people saw the ad. Post 01 Reel should hit 15,000+ over 7 days on $200.
CPM
Cost per 1,000 impressions. Under $15 is good for this region. Reels typically run $5–12 CPM.
Video Views (Reel)
Watch the "50% views" column — this is your retargeting pool building in real time.
Cost Per Lead
Target: $15–25. If CPL goes above $30 after 3+ days, pause Post 04 and check troubleshooting below.
Leads
Actual form submissions. At $15–25 CPL and $300 spend you're targeting 12–20 leads total from Post 04.
1
Open Ads Manager → check the Delivery column. All active ads should show "Active". If any show "Learning" for more than 3–4 days, that's normal — just wait.
2
For Post 04 (Lead Gen): check the Leads and Cost per Lead columns daily from Day 2 onwards.
3
Download leads daily: Meta Business Suite → Instant Forms → your form → Download. Call new leads same day if possible.
4
Don't touch the ads in the first 48 hours — the algorithm needs time to find its feet. Editing an ad in the first 2 days resets the learning phase.
Pause if
Post 04 CPL > $30
After 3+ days with no improvement, pause the ad. Check the audience size — if the custom audience is very small, widen to the broader homeowner targeting and relaunch.
Ad rejected
Read the rejection reason in Ads Manager. Most common fix: remove trigger words like "you" + a personal attribute ("your erosion problem"), or reduce text on the image.
Increase if
Post 01 CPL < $8
If the Reel is reaching people very cheaply in the first 2–3 days, consider adding $50–100 to it. More Reel viewers = bigger retarget pool for Week 2.
Post 04 CPL < $15
If leads are coming in under $15 each, there's budget headroom. Add $50–100 to Post 04 if there's remaining campaign budget.
1
Go to Meta Business Suite (business.facebook.com)
2
Click Instant Forms in the left menu (under Advertising)
3
Find your form → click Download → CSV file opens with all lead details: name, phone, suburb
4
Call each lead within 1 business day — lead response rate drops sharply after 24 hours
💡
You can also connect Meta leads directly to a CRM or Google Sheet via Zapier — if Truehold gets volume, this is worth setting up so leads land automatically without manual CSV downloads.
